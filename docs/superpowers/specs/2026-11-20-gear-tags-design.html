HOW IT GETS BUILT

Approaches considered

With my pick, tagged pieces also flip to out or in on the Serials page, so Rentals stays accurate without a second scan.

Scope

Not in this build

  • Tags on individual cables, stands and adapters. They stay counted by quantity.
  • Location tracking, or anything customers can see.
  • Changes to estimates, invoices or pricing.

Would make it a failure

  • Load-out gets slower than the paper or tap method.
  • A scan needs a signal the venue does not have and loses the check.
  • A stranger who scans a tag learns anything about LRE gear.

Comes with every hub change

  • One database migration, pasted in full for you to run.
